A soda can wall is about as thin as paper. So why doesn't it burst? Inside, the fizz pushes hard. Up to 90 pounds per square inch. That's almost three times your car tire. Now flip the can over. The bottom isn't flat. It curves inward, like a dome. Plot twist. The real reason is the arch. A flat bottom would bulge out. The can would rock and wobble. A dome works like a stone arch. Pressure pushes on it... and the shape holds. A flat bottom would need a lot more metal. The bottom metal is also about three times thicker than the walls. Same trick as old bridges and dams. So the smartest part of a soda can... is the part you never look at.
