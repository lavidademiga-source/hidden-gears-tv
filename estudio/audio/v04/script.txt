Close your eyes. You can still find F and J. Two tiny bumps are doing that. Myth: they're only there for blind users. Plot twist. The real reason is for everyone who types. Your fingers have a home on the middle row. Left index finger on F. Right index finger on J. From there, every other letter is one short reach away. The bumps let you find home by touch. No looking down. No typing a whole line one key off. Reality: they help anyone type without looking. Even the number pad has one, right on the five. Same trick, for numbers. Your phone screen has no bumps. That's why you keep looking down. So next time you type in the dark... thank two tiny bumps. Now close your eyes.
