Wrong side of the pump again? Your car already told you. Look at your fuel gauge. See that tiny triangle next to the pump icon? It points to the side your gas cap is on. Left arrow, left side. Right arrow, right side. Plot twist. The real reason it exists is one soggy designer. In 1986, a Ford designer named Jim Moylan grabbed a company car. It was raining. He had no idea which side the cap was on. So he wrote a memo: put an arrow by the gauge. Then he forgot about it. Three years later, it showed up on 1989 Ford dashboards. Today, you'll find it on most new cars. No apps. No guessing. Just one tiny triangle. So next time you pull up... check the triangle first.
