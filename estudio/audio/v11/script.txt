What if the gas pump never clicked off? Fuel would spill everywhere. But it stops on its own. No electronics needed. Plot twist. The real reason is a tiny hole. Near the tip of the nozzle, there's a little air hole. In one classic design, it's about 1/16 of an inch. While you pump, rushing fuel sucks air through it. As long as air flows, nothing happens. But when fuel reaches the tip... it covers the hole. No more air. The suction builds up instantly. It pulls on a rubber disc inside the handle. And that trips a latch. Click. The nozzle shuts. Just air, fuel, and a spring. That's why it works even when you look away. So that click... is your tank saying full.
